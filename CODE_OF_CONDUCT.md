# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is committed to a friendly, safe and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background or identity. Contributing here should be a harassment-free experience.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the community: being respectful, patient and welcoming; giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback; focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information without permission, and conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits) and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer. The preferred route is the repository's **Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**, which is a private channel. You can also contact the owner, **Rahul Krishna ([@Rahul-Innv](https://github.com/Rahul-Innv))**, directly on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may take any action they judge appropriate, up to a temporary or permanent ban. For serious abuse, use GitHub's [report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow.
